BUILT AROUND YOUR BOOK

Good work begins with a clear brief, honest expectations, and a shared understanding of what success looks like.

What the process can include

  • Confirm audiobook rights and permissions
  • Estimate finished hours and production budget
  • Agree pronunciation notes and performance style
  • Verify current platform specifications before delivery
Narration, studio, editing, and distribution costs vary. Availability and platform requirements should be confirmed before a production schedule is agreed.
