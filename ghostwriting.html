BUILT AROUND YOUR BOOK

Good work begins with a clear brief, honest expectations, and a shared understanding of what success looks like.

What the process can include

  • Discovery conversations to capture your perspective
  • An agreed outline before substantial drafting
  • Structured reviews and feedback milestones
  • Revision focused on clarity, consistency, and your voice
Your experience remains at the centre. The scope, working rhythm, ownership terms, and deliverables should be agreed in writing before work begins.
