BUILT AROUND YOUR BOOK

Good work begins with a clear brief, honest expectations, and a shared understanding of what success looks like.

What the process can include

  • Discuss references, mood, and audience expectations
  • Align the visual direction before detailed production
  • Review readability at thumbnail and full size
  • Check dimensions and technical specifications with the printer or platform
Final deliverables, revision rounds, source files, and licensing for any fonts or imagery should be stated in the project agreement.
