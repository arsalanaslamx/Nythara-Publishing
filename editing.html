BUILT AROUND YOUR BOOK

Good work begins with a clear brief, honest expectations, and a shared understanding of what success looks like.

What the process can include

  • Early drafts may need structural feedback first
  • Near-final manuscripts may benefit from copyediting
  • Proofreading is best after substantive revisions are complete
  • A sample review can help clarify scope before a full project
Editing levels are distinct. A proofreading engagement should not be treated as a substitute for developmental editing or a full rewrite.
